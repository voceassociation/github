A practical bridge between support and deployment.

VOCE is developing a regional cooperation pathway with a clear purpose: help turn identified health needs into projects that institutions, foundations and technology providers can assess and support. Each project begins with a local institution, its clinical priorities and the resources required to sustain delivery.

The four countries define the intended regional scope. Hosting institutions, budgets, responsibilities and deployment timetables are to be agreed project by project.

Public project briefs

These summaries draw on VOCE’s Medical Impact 2030 and institutional-financing working papers. Detailed papers and financial assumptions are available for institutional discussion on request.

VOCE / 01

Oncology & therapeutic technologies

Purpose
Support the assessment of oncology technologies, imaging workflows and access needs with the institutions responsible for care.
Support sought
Equipment funding or loans, technical assessment, installation, team training and maintenance planning.
Project dossier
A clinical need, an accountable host institution, a proposed solution and its full operating costs.
VOCE / 02

Psychiatry & psycho-oncology

Purpose
Help develop professional education, mental-health cooperation and coordinated support around the experience of cancer.
Support sought
Training, specialist cooperation, translation and assessment of appropriate tools with local teams.
Project dossier
A locally agreed programme, qualified clinical leadership and measurable delivery objectives.
VOCE / 03

Knowledge & technical deployment

Purpose
Build the practical capacity needed for a funded technology or programme to remain useful over time.
Support sought
Scientific education, technical skills, multilingual resources, connectivity and implementation support.
Project dossier
A delivery plan covering staff, infrastructure, training, service continuity and evaluation.

Before deployment

  1. A documented need and a named local institutional lead.
  2. An agreed clinical and technical scope, with the necessary local permissions.
  3. A budget covering equipment, staffing, training, maintenance and recurring costs.
  4. Written responsibilities, milestones and indicators agreed before funding is committed.

Working papers: Medical Impact 2030 and Expanded 5-Year CAPEX/OPEX & EU Finance, revised 19 October 2026. Proposed programmes; financing and institutional arrangements remain to be agreed.

Clear responsibilities. Independent judgement.

VOCE’s role is to develop cooperation, assemble evidence, coordinate contributors and support funding applications. Clinical decisions and treatment remain with the qualified professionals and institutions responsible for care. Funding relationships should be transparent and preserve scientific independence.
