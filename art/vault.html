Private access

Nothing sensitive is public here.

The Vault is the controlled documentary layer of VOCE Art. Individual access credentials are issued only for authorised dossiers.

No artwork, provenance file, confidential image or ownership document is exposed on this public entry page. Sensitive material will be added only after the relevant dossier, rights of circulation and access perimeter have been defined.

For an authorised access request, contact contact@voce.life.

Principle

Discretion before visibility.

The existence of a work does not create an obligation to publish it. VOCE Art treats confidentiality as part of the architecture of the relationship, not as an afterthought.
