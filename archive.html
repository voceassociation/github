Academy

A corpus, not a feed.

Each subject becomes an autonomous documentary unit: a permanent address, full text, date, provenance and a place in a knowledge architecture.

VOCE Academy progressively turns editorial publications into an educational library. Chronology remains available, but it is no longer the primary structure. A reader can enter through an AI mechanism, a disease, a question of cognition or a cultural object, then move through the corpus without depending on a social platform.

VOCE Guide

Ask the corpus.

Describe a subject, study, technology or question. VOCE Guide searches Academy publications and returns the documents that support its answer.

Answers are grounded in the VOCE corpus. If the corpus does not contain the information, the guide must say so.
