      Before you begin

      Is this a service staffed by lawyers?

      LEGIS offers documentary guidance and preparation tools. No VOCE legal professional reviews your account in this pathway. For advice on your situation, contact a qualified professional or legal advice service.

      Do I need to pay or create an account?

      This guide is free and requires no account. Each linked service explains its own conditions. A free first consultation does not mean all subsequent proceedings will be free.

      What happens to my answers?

      Fields are processed in your browser. LEGIS does not save them or send them to an AI. Reloading can clear them. A downloaded file stays on your device; you choose whether to share it. The website and external sites have their own privacy information.

      Can I use it outside France?

      The resources concern France. The English and Italian versions translate this pathway; they do not provide English or Italian legal services. For another country, the sheet only helps organise the facts.
