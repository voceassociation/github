01

Formal research

Formal research outputs use stable authorship, references, versioning and persistent identifiers where appropriate. Current work includes parental technoference and early language development, and the relationship between continuous digital stimulation and inner thinking.

The purpose is not volume. It is to create a small body of work whose method and claims can be inspected.

02

Applied research

Applied research follows mechanisms into operating environments. In AI governance, that means moving beyond model benchmarks to permissions, agents, RAG, tools, security, observability, FinOps and human accountability. In work, it means examining how agents change task architecture, productivity, apprenticeship and expertise.

In health and human behaviour, it means connecting biomarkers, diagnostics, attention, memory, relationships, platform design and lived experience without confusing public analysis with clinical care.

03

Editorial intelligence

VOCE’s editorial work is a research interface. It translates recent studies, technical systems, infrastructures and institutional changes into compact analysis without turning them into simplified news.

The recurring editorial test is: “I knew the subject, but not this.” The value lies in exposing a mechanism or detail that changes the reader’s mental model.

04

COURS VOCE®

COURS VOCE® is the explanatory layer. A course isolates one mechanism, defines the architecture, names the relevant metrics or controls, and shows how the subject connects to a wider system.

The format is deliberately compact. It is designed to be useful to readers who need more than a headline but less than a full academic paper.

05

Research geography

VOCE’s research is informed by different operating ecosystems. Europe provides dense regulatory and institutional contexts. Dubai provides a Gulf perspective shaped by infrastructure and rapid adoption. Hangzhou, Shanghai and Hong Kong expose different Chinese technology, commercial, industrial and financial systems.

Comparison is used to test assumptions, not to manufacture superficial internationalism.

06

Research agenda

The current agenda spans AI governance, sovereignty and orchestration, light workforce, technoference, cognition, attention, memory, health and medicine, education, culture, heritage, infrastructure and the changing relationship between people and machines.

These are not independent verticals. VOCE treats them as interacting layers of one contemporary system.
