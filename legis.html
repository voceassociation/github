What is available today

Can I use a confidential case file?

This beta is for fictional or anonymised examples. It does not yet provide private case spaces or guaranteed European residency for inference.

Is the note a legal opinion?

The note organises sources, questions and points for examination. It does not certify a contract’s compliance or an institution’s legal classification.

What happens to the VOCE corpus?

Academy, courses and the corpus remain available. LEGIS adds a workshop to the site and is the first pilot of the VOCE aggregator.
