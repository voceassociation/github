Learners would work through practical tasks and build a portfolio. Pedagogical agents are intended to explain concepts and comment on exercises within a defined curriculum, under the responsibility of authorised human educators.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Experience in vocational education</h2><p>The design draws on team members with experience in GRETA and AFPA vocational education environments. Their individual experience informs observable objectives and professional situations; it does not constitute a partnership with those organisations.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Six programmes for development</h2><div class="courses-grid"><article class="course-card"><h3>IT Foundations</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Everyday computer use.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Write and explain a simple program; understand files, processes and networks.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>Variables, functions, command line, files and troubleshooting. Build a documented utility.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>40–60 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>New test cases, program explanation and independent diagnosis.</dd></dl></article><article class="course-card"><h3>Software Engineering</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Basic programming and command line.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Build maintainable software and verify behaviour.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>Version control, modularity, tests, review and integration. Build a small service with error handling.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>60–90 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>Code repository, reproducible tests, human review and regression repair.</dd></dl></article><article class="course-card"><h3>Data &amp; APIs</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Basic programming.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Model data and integrate systems with integrity checks.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>SQL, transactions, API contracts, authentication and idempotency. Build a recoverable synchronisation.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>50–70 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>Schema, queries, contract tests, duplicates and simulated failure.</dd></dl></article><article class="course-card"><h3>Cloud &amp; Cybersecurity</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Systems and network foundations.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Operate a service with permissions, monitoring and recovery.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>Identity, deployment, secrets, backups and incidents. Prepare an isolated service and restore exercise.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>60–90 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>Reviewable configuration, access denials, restoration and fictional incident report.</dd></dl></article><article class="course-card"><h3>AI &amp; Agent Engineering</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Programming, APIs and statistical foundations.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Build and evaluate an agent with bounded permissions.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>Qwen and accessible-weight models, licences, retrieval, tools, MCP and governance. Use fictional data.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>60–90 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>Separate evaluation set, documented errors, tool controls, cost per accepted task and human review.</dd></dl></article><article class="course-card"><h3>Enterprise Systems</h3><dl><dt>Prerequisites</dt><dd>Data and business processes.</dd><dt>Objectives and skills</dt><dd>Map and automate an ERP/CRM workflow with business controls.</dd><dt>Progression and practice</dt><dd>Data models, permissions, reconciliation, exceptions and exit planning. Build a test integration.</dd><dt>Proposed duration</dt><dd>50–80 hours</dd><dt>Assessment</dt><dd>Process map, reconciliation, denial tests and recovery procedure.</dd></dl></article></div><p>Durations are design estimates to refine with the institution. These programmes are development proposals; enrolment and recognised qualifications are not offered on this page.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Virtual teachers within explicit boundaries</h2><p>The target architecture would support text and voice in Montenegrin, English, Italian and French, progress tracking and isolated programming exercises. These capabilities require a pilot demonstration. Feedback must identify its criteria and allow a human educator to review and correct it.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Accessibility and support</h2><p>The pilot should provide keyboard access, structured text, voice transcripts, alternative activities where needed, adjusted timing and a human contact. Accommodations will be agreed before opening.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Documented learning, defined recognition</h2><p>A module and attendance statement document participation. Skills assessment requires reviewable outputs and criteria. A recognised certification or qualification depends on the competent authority. A degree requires a legally authorised institution and appropriate agreements and permissions. These proposals are not currently awarded degrees.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Frameworks and scope</h2><p>e-CF / EN 16234-1:2019 supports professional ICT competence design; DigComp 3.0 supports transversal digital skills; ESCO identifies occupations and skills. Montenegro's national framework and vocational or university requirements must be examined for the relevant institution and programme. ISO 21001:2025 is a voluntary management reference, not a certification claim. Detailed mappings require documentation before programme validation.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>FR · EN · IT · Montenegrin</h2><p>Technical language will require a versioned glossary, review by a competent native speaker with subject knowledge and learner comprehension testing. Montenegrin terminology requires dedicated validation. No Montenegrin edition is currently presented as available.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Concept · Proposed programmes and architecture</h2><p>Concept → development → pilot → verified deployment. Moving between stages requires deliverables and evidence; no pilot or government partnership is presented as established.</p></section><section class="wrap courses-section"><h2>Institutional sources consulted on 18 October 2026</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2022/eninthespotlight/2022-04-01-new-technical-specification-ict-education-europe/">e-CF / EN 16234-1:2019</a></li><li><a href="https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/scientific-activities/key-competences-lifelong-learning/digital-competence-framework-digcomp/digcomp-30_en">DigComp 3.0</a></li><li><a href="https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en">ESCO</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.me/en/documents/0dd46168-c2ce-4e26-aadd-ae51da41e41a">Montenegro · National Qualifications Framework</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.me/en/mps/legislative-and-strategic-framework-for-the-areas-of-education-science-and-innovation">Montenegro · Education legislation</a></li><li><a href="https://www.iso.org/standard/21001">ISO 21001:2025</a></li></ul></section><nav class="wrap courses-section" aria-label="Resources"><a href="/applied/education-systems">Education Systems</a> · <a href="/en/cours">Academy catalogue</a> · <a href="/agent-infrastructure">Agent infrastructure</a> · <a href="/agent-conformance">Evaluation and controls</a><p><a href="mailto:contact@voce.life">Discuss a pilot</a></p></nav></main><footer><div class="wrap"><div class="footer"><a class="voce-mark" href="/" aria-label="VOCE"><img src="/voce-wordmark.svg" alt="" width="92" height="28"></a><div class="footer-right"><div>Paris · London · Dubai · Hangzhou · Shanghai · Hong Kong</div><div><a href="/archive">VOCE Academy</a> · <a href="/atlas">VOCE Atlas</a> · <a href="/applied">VOCE Applied</a> · <a href="/research">Research</a></div><div>Copyright © 2025-2026 VOCE Association.
