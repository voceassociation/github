The Atlas

Not an index. A map of mechanisms.

An article belongs to more than one category. A discussion of AI agents can also be about identity, permissions and work. A study of a doorway can also be about event segmentation, interruption and memory.

Atlas preserves those crossings. Select a concept to reveal its documented relationships, or choose two concepts to trace the shortest evidence-linked path between them.

Method

A relation must earn its line.

The first Atlas is deliberately selective. It does not draw a connection because two words appear in the same corpus. A displayed relation must be supported by a VOCE publication or formal research record.

Source
Every relation links back to evidence in VOCE Academy or VOCE Research.
Scope
The map begins with a curated core rather than automatically visualising the entire archive.
Growth
New concepts can be proposed automatically, but publication into Atlas remains evidence-bound.
Path semantics
A path is a documentary sequence across individually supported relations. It does not automatically establish causality between its endpoints.
Machine-readable
The Atlas graph is publicly inspectable as JSON.
