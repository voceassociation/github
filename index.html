VOCE studies the systems that are changing human life before their consequences become obvious: artificial intelligence, work, attention, knowledge, health and culture.

Current research lines

Four questions running through VOCE.

01

AI governance

VOCE examines the full operating architecture of AI: data, models, agents, tools, permissions, RAG, APIs, identity, observability and human responsibility. The central issue is no longer only what a model can generate, but what an AI system may access, decide, execute, retain and revoke once it becomes part of an institution.

Topics developed in VOCE courses: agents, RAG, API/IAM/MCP, model routing, AI FinOps, security, procurement, regulation and human oversight.
02

Open AI, sovereignty & orchestration

Open models and multi-model stacks make the nationality of the model different from the nationality of the service. A European platform may orchestrate American, European or Chinese models through APIs. VOCE follows the governance consequences: data sovereignty, control planes, model choice, portability, dependency and the ability to change or revoke components without rebuilding the whole system.

The question is not whether a model is “open” in isolation, but who controls the architecture in which it operates.
03

Light workforce

VOCE uses “light workforce” to describe organisations whose economic scale grows faster than their permanent payroll. AI agents, software automation and robotics absorb tasks once distributed across large teams, while humans concentrate on judgment, exceptions, maintenance, control and responsibility. The gain in productivity creates another risk: if entry-level tasks disappear, the organisation can also erase the apprenticeship through which future experts were formed.

The subject spans offices, banks, healthcare software, factories, logistics and autonomous digital workforces.
04

Technoference

Technoference describes the interruptions introduced by connected devices into attention, relationships and internal thought. VOCE studies it from early childhood to adult cognition: parent-child interaction, language development, fragmented attention, cognitive offloading, social validation, screenshots used as external memory, and the gradual transfer of remembering, choosing and anticipating to digital systems.

The recurring question is simple: what happens to human attention when interruption becomes the default environment?

Method

Research before posture.

Recent evidence

Priority to current studies, technical documentation, cohorts, regulations, infrastructure and primary sources.

Mechanisms

Explain how a system works, not only what happened: architecture, protocol, incentives, constraints and human consequences.

Limits

Separate correlation from causality, documented facts from interpretation, and formal research from editorial analysis.

Human effects

Technology is studied together with attention, expertise, decision-making, institutions, work and culture.

Scientific committee

Expertise close to the systems it studies.

VOCE draws on a scientific committee and expert network whose members work inside the ecosystems being analysed. The approach is deliberately transnational and interdisciplinary: technology and artificial intelligence are read alongside psychiatry, culture, art, philanthropy and institutional practice.

Position
Observe from within the ecosystems, compare across regions, and separate evidence from posture.

This geography matters. Paris and London bring institutional, financial and cultural depth; Dubai connects fast-moving Gulf ecosystems; Hangzhou, Shanghai and Hong Kong place VOCE close to Chinese technology, commerce and innovation environments. VOCE uses these vantage points to compare systems rather than treat one market as the default model for the world.

No one left unheard.

VOCE is an independent French non-profit association based in Paris and connected to expert ecosystems in London, Dubai and China. It develops research, scientific and cultural mediation, public education and editorial work on contemporary transformations affecting human life.
