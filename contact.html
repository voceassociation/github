Research & expertise

For research dialogue, scientific committee exchanges, source corrections or expert contribution, contact VOCE with the subject area and the nature of the contribution.

VOCE prioritises exchanges that add evidence, challenge a mechanism or provide direct knowledge of an ecosystem.

03

Institutional & cultural work

VOCE can engage with institutions, foundations and cultural organisations around research, seminars, scientific and cultural mediation, AI governance, digital environments and the human consequences of technological change.

The approach remains research-led rather than promotional.
