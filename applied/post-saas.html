Decide which layers the organisation should control

A subscription provides access to a service. Replacing it transfers maintenance, security and continuity obligations to the organisation. VOCE examines that transfer before recommending change. The assessment may support retaining the existing system, targeted integration or replacement of a clearly bounded scope.

Define what can be replaced

CRM and client relationships

Map customer data, access rights, commercial workflows and integrations. Test historical data recovery and synchronisation rules before moving a process.

ERP and critical operations

Identify the functions concerned and their constraints. Accounting entries, inventory, payments and approvals remain governed by deterministic rules and explicit controls.

APIs connect applications. MCP can provide an assistant with controlled access to tools. Neither replaces business authorisation: a generated suggestion does not, by itself, become an approved transaction.

Enterprise systems across business and institutional settings

Museums and foundations

Ticketing, audience and donor relationships, distribution and reconciliation. Define the data that remains under institutional control and the exchanges required with partners.

Hotels and hospitality groups

Connect the property management system (PMS), CRM and booking channels. Examine guest journeys, duplicate entry and synchronisation rules before replacing a function.

OTAs and distributors

For online travel agencies and their partners: availability, rates, commissions, cancellations, refunds and reconciliation. Connections depend on available APIs and distribution agreements.

Accounting and legal operations

Assist or automate bounded tasks: document preparation, reconciliations, documentary research and clause comparison. Accounting entries, payments and consequential conclusions follow approvals defined with the responsible specialists.

Scope is built around each organisation’s processes. These examples describe engagements to be assessed; they are neither a catalogue of available products nor client references.

Deliverables before a deployment commitment

01 · Assessment

Functional and contractual inventory, data map, cost comparison and retained responsibilities. Deliverable: a decision brief that also documents reasons not to migrate.

02 · Bounded pilot

One process, authorised data and measurable acceptance criteria. Deliverable: test results, observed costs and conditions for reverting to the original system.

03 · Controlled deployment

Migration, data reconciliation, training and a cutover conditional on acceptance. Deliverable: operating procedures, service commitments and an exit plan.

Replace a component without rebuilding the system

The transactional database holds business state. Identity and access management (IAM) limits permissions. Documented interfaces and logs make actions traceable. Backups and recovery are tested. AI inference is a separate, replaceable layer evaluated against the selected tasks.

Qwen, Alibaba’s model family, and other models with accessible weights may be assessed against their licences, performance and hosting constraints. A model choice determines neither the hosting provider nor data location. Private and dedicated deployments are options to be assessed.

LÉGIS · this architecture, implemented by VOCE

VOCE already applies this separation of functions in LÉGIS, its beta legal service. A business interface calls an API running on Cloudflare Workers. A curated catalogue of official sources constrains generation; rules check references and response structure. Cloudflare Workers AI runs Qwen and GLM models. A D1 database manages quotas and operating metrics. These components illustrate an architecture distinct from the AI model, with service-specific controls. The current legal scope covers France and the European Union; this example is not an ERP/CRM deployment reference.

What the model includes

Investment is counted once at the start. Annual increases apply in months 13 and 25. During coexistence, the alternative bears its own costs plus current subscriptions and retained legacy administration. Retained licences must exclude subscriptions already counted during coexistence. The annual table includes investment in year one; cumulative curves include it at month zero.

The model assigns no value to productivity gains, additional revenue or the cost of capital. Exit penalties and supplier fees must be included in the relevant inputs. Incidents and migration risk are not priced. Results are rounded to whole euros; the CSV retains two decimals. A favourable difference is not a guaranteed saving.

Assign responsibility before cutover

The mission must identify the business owner, operator and people authorised to approve a critical action. Security, confidentiality and exit conditions are documented before the pilot. For an international organisation, scoping identifies jurisdictions, data location and transfers, working languages, support hours and local rules to integrate. Training prepares teams to operate the system and resume a process during an outage.
