VOCE Scholar

Research designed to remain findable, citable and inspectable.

VOCE Scholar is the formal research layer of VOCE Association. It separates scholarly outputs from the wider editorial corpus and gives each eligible work stable bibliographic metadata, persistent identifiers and a permanent institutional URL.
