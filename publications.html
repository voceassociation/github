02

Editorial corpus

VOCE’s editorial corpus spans artificial intelligence, health, neuroscience, psychology, technology, infrastructure, culture, logistics, medicine, education and human behaviour. These texts are not presented as peer-reviewed papers unless they are formal research outputs.

Their function is interpretive: identify an overlooked mechanism, explain why it matters and preserve the chain back to the source.

03

COURS VOCE®

The course format converts a difficult subject into an expert explanatory unit: mechanism, architecture, vocabulary, controls, metrics and implications.

Topics increasingly include AI systems, governance, agents, RAG, model routing, sovereignty, infrastructure, workforce transformation and the cognitive effects of digital environments.

04

Versions and archive

VOCE is building a canonical institutional archive so that research outputs, courses and major editorial texts can be located by subject, date, source and version.

The website is intended to become that source of record rather than leaving the institution dependent on social platforms.
