Privacy policy

How VOCE handles personal data and technical information associated with voce.life.

VOCE
LegalPrivacyStandardsContact
01

Data collected

VOCE does not require an account to read the website. Personal data may be received when a visitor contacts VOCE directly by email or voluntarily provides information in another exchange.

02

Purpose and retention

Contact data is used to respond to enquiries, research exchanges, institutional requests or rights-related matters. It is retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose or applicable legal obligations.

03

Technical processing

Cloudflare may process technical data necessary to deliver, secure and protect the website, including network and request information. The site currently does not embed advertising trackers or behavioural marketing tools.

04

Language preference

The EN/FR/IT selector stores the visitor’s chosen language locally in the browser so the preference can be reused. This preference is functional and is not used by VOCE for advertising profiling.

05

External resources

The website may load typography resources from Google Fonts and may link to external services such as LinkedIn, Zenodo DOI records and other referenced institutions. Those services apply their own privacy policies when visited or loaded.

06

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, restriction, objection or deletion of personal data concerning them. Requests can be sent to contact@voce.life. In France, individuals may also contact the CNIL.

07

Contact

Privacy enquiries: contact@voce.life.
