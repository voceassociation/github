Welcome to VOCE courses. Choose a course from the catalogue: it will open on a dedicated page. Read the objective and the explanation, then work on the proposed case before opening the answer. Each course contains fewer than five thousand characters, along with its references. You can follow the ten steps of the first track, or start with the subject useful to your company. For training centres, the evaluation criteria indicate what the participant must be able to do. The themes of the future Campus are presented separately from the courses already available. They will also cover gigafactories, robotics, and the light workforce.

Funders and training centres

A demonstrator that you can examine

The first track presents ten observable objectives, written courses, and reasoned answers. Each course indicates an evaluation criterion and a production to be kept.

A center can use it for autonomous reading with guided correction, then adapt the situations to its participants' professions.

Verify acquired skills

Before the track, collect a justified decision on a case. After the track, propose a different case of comparable difficulty. A deferred exercise allows for examining reuse.

Pedagogical results will be documented during a pilot. The demonstrator does not claim any external certification.
